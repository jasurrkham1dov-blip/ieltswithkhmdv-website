SECTION 2

Questions 11-15

Hi, everybody. Thank you very much for agreeing to participate in the next beach clean-up day. These days are a real benefit for our community. I'm Brenda, the organiser, and I just want to explain a bit about the day to help you prepare and to answer any questions you might have.

The beach that's been chosen for the clean-up day is Quinlan Beach, because it's easily accessible by public transport. The beach is really popular and well used by locals and tourists alike. The town council does its best to provide bins, but litter is still a problem. It's important to deal with this, as it's a threat to the groups of seals that swim around there and regularly rest on the flat rocks. When you observe them in their natural habitat, you really sense why you're picking up this litter.

You'll be working in groups of three to four people at the beach. Some of you have signed up as groups already, which is great. The rest of you will be organised into groups on the day. There are different roles in the group. It'll be up to you to choose who does what in the group. After lunch, we'll form new groups, which will give you a chance to get to know new people.

You may be wondering what kind of equipment you'll need. You may have seen tools to pick up litter with. They're like poles with a sharp end. If you want to use one of those, you'll have to bring your own. After the safety briefing, we'll give you each a whistle to blow in case you need to get our attention. The town council is providing special collection bags for us to use, which are fully biodegradable.

There are a few things to keep in mind regarding clothing for the day. Definitely go for something comfortable and wear shoes or boots that will keep your feet dry. We're all going to be given yellow caps so we can identify each other. The town council is supplying vinyl gloves, but they're quite large, so your own may be more comfortable. Sunscreen is up to you, but highly recommended.

Each group that goes out picking up litter will be given a survey to fill in as you go along. The town council can get an estimate of the quantity and the different kinds of litter we gather up by examining the bags we collect. However, they are very keen to know more about where you find the rubbish, which may help them in future. So that's what you're asked to indicate on the survey.

Questions 16-20

OK, now please look at the map showing Quinlan Beach. When you arrive, you can park in the car park, which is at the bottom of the map.

The first aid station is close to the shore and will be staffed all day in case anyone needs it. You can find it by taking the footpath that goes north from the car park towards the beach. The path branches out, but carry on straight and there it is.

To get to the equipment pick-up, walk along the road that leads from the car park, around the bend and past the bus stop. There's a small building before you get to the golf club and that's where the equipment is kept.

You'll want to know where the toilets are. They're adjacent to the car park, on the opposite side from where the wooded area is.

You can see the beach is sort of divided into two stretches by Rocky Point. The meeting point for the beach clean-up is on the beach that's closest to the cafe. Later in the day, you'll move to the West Beach.

We'll be meeting for lunch at 1pm. Some food is available in the cafe or you can bring your own. We'll meet in the area immediately outside the entrance to the Grand Pier where there are picnic tables. You get a good view from there. So now, if there are any questions...
