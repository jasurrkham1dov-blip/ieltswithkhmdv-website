Saving the Skink What can be done to save the skink, one of New Zealand's most endangered lizards? New Zealand’s skinks (over 90 species) face predation, parasite infections, and habitat loss. Grand and Otago skinks mature late and have few young, making them especially vulnerable. DOC efforts like predator control and mammal‑proof fencing are underway, while captive breeding is a last resort. Public understanding and multi‑stakeholder projects are crucial. ...
